# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve Awesome Internet Trends! To suggest a resource, open a pull request that edits `README.md`.

## Guidelines

- **Free and open access only.** The resource must be usable without paying. Paid tools, or tools that require a subscription to see trend data, will not be accepted.
- **Pick the right section:** Open-source Projects, Social Media Trends or General Trend Analytics.
- **Use this format:**

  ```
  - [Name](https://url) - One-sentence description. **Free and open to access.**
  ```

- **One resource per pull request,** with a short note on why it's useful.
- **Check for duplicates** before adding something.
- **If you add or rename a section,** update the Contents list at the top of the README.
